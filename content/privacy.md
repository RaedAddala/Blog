This is a personal website. I don't sell or share data with anyone, and I use visit statistics
only to see which pages people read and how they found them.

## Cloudflare Web Analytics (always on)

Every page loads [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/), which counts
visits without cookies or local storage and without fingerprinting the visitor. It records the page
address, the referring site, the browser, operating system and device type, page load timings, and
an approximate country. See
[Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Google Analytics (only if you accept)

Google Analytics is loaded only after you click **Accept** in the banner. Until then, no request is
sent to Google and no Google cookies are set. If your browser sends **Do Not Track** or
**Global Privacy Control**, the banner is not shown and Google Analytics never loads.

When you accept:

- Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_<id>`) that last up to two years.
- It receives the pages you view, the referring site, campaign parameters in the address
  (`utm_source` and similar), your browser, device and approximate location.
- Advertising features, ad personalisation and Google signals are turned off.
- If you accepted on a later page than the one you arrived on, the address, title and referrer of
  that first page are sent too, so the visit is attributed to the right source. Until you decide,
  they stay in your browser tab's session storage and nothing is sent; they are deleted as soon as
  you choose either option, or when you close the tab.

See [how Google uses this data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choice

Your answer is saved in your browser's local storage (`analytics-consent`) and you'll be asked
again after about six months. You can change it at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.
Choosing **Reject** stops Google Analytics on the spot and deletes its cookies.

## Comments

Blog posts with comments enabled load [giscus](https://giscus.app/), which stores comments in GitHub
Discussions. Commenting requires signing in with GitHub, and
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
applies.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/about-github-pages#data-collection),
which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.
