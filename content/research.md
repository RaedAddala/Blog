## Current: PhD on caching for massively distributed systems

I am a PhD student in the [MAGELLAN](https://team.inria.fr/magellan/) team at Inria and IRISA in Rennes, France. My thesis is about **caching for massively distributed systems**.

MAGELLAN is an Inria / IRISA project-team working on reliable and responsible decentralized computing infrastructures: making them efficient, robust, environment-friendly, and rigorously evaluated.

## Earlier research

### Federated learning in Fog computing (LaBRI, 2026)

During a research internship at [LaBRI](https://www.labri.fr/) in Bordeaux (March to August 2026), I designed a federated learning approach that addresses statistical and system heterogeneity in Fog computing environments, and developed a validation suite to evaluate it under diverse data, compute, and network conditions.

### Parallel synchronization for PBFT-based IoT blockchains (2025)

An academic research project extending [RC-PBFT](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10586-024-04923-4), a random-cluster parallel PBFT consensus, with a synchronization module and an unordered global distributed transaction algorithm for high-throughput IoT applications. It led to a [journal publication](/publications/). Details on the [project page](/projects/rc-pbft/).
